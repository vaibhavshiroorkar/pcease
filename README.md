# PCease

**Build your perfect PC — without the guesswork.**

PCease helps you put together a desktop PC that fits your budget and actually works
together, using real prices from Indian stores. No jargon, no sign-up needed, always free.

Live at **[pcease.vercel.app](https://pcease.vercel.app)**.

## What you can do

- **Ask the AI** — Tell it your budget and what you'll use the PC for ("a ₹60,000 gaming PC"),
  and it picks real parts that fit together, checks they're compatible, and shows you a build
  you can actually buy today.
- **Browse parts** — Explore CPUs, graphics cards, memory, storage and more, with prices
  compared across multiple Indian retailers so you always see the best deal.
- **Build it yourself** — Add parts slot by slot, watch your budget and power draw update live,
  and get a heads-up if two parts won't work together.
- **Compare** — Put parts side by side to see which is the better pick.
- **See price trends** — A simple chart shows how a part's price has moved over the past
  day, week, or month.
- **Ask the community** — A forum to get advice from other PC builders in India.

## Made for India

Every price is in ₹ from Indian stores — no dollar conversions or guesswork. PCease is free
to use and doesn't need an account to browse, compare, or get AI recommendations.

## Want to run it yourself?

PCease is open source. If you're a developer and want to run or contribute to it, see the
**[Developer Guide](DEVELOPMENT.md)** — it covers setup, the AI agent, the API, and the
architecture. You can even run the whole thing locally with built-in sample data, no cloud
accounts required.
